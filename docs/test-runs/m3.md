## 3. PRD cost example walk-through (TC-RE-30)

Goal: prove the app reproduces PRD section 6 and section 4.1 end to end. Run it on one iOS and one Android device, with airplane mode on.

### 3.1 Prices to have in the Pantry

The seed already holds these. If the seed was edited, set them by hand: Pantry, tap the item, Add or Update price. Check each item's unit cost line.

| Item | Package price and size | Unit cost the app must show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bread flour | $12.00 for 5,000 g | $0.24 per 100 g |
| Whole wheat flour | $7.00 for 2,000 g | $0.35 per 100 g |
| Salt | $1.00 for 1,000 g | $0.10 per 100 g |
| Water | $0.00 for 1,000 ml | Free |

### 3.2 Build the recipe by hand

1. Recipes tab, New recipe. If recipes exist, choose Blank. Name it `PRD loaf`.
2. Add ingredient, pick **Bread flour**, amount `450`, unit g, Done. The row reads Bread flour, 450 g.
3. Add **Whole wheat flour**, `50` g.
4. Add **Salt**, `10` g.
5. Add **Water**, `375` g.
6. Add ingredient, choose **Levain (starter)**. The sheet that opens is titled "Levain is fed with". Pick **Bread flour**, amount `100`, hydration stays `100`. Below the field it must read "100 g levain = 50 g flour + 50 g water." and "Its 50 g of flour costs $0.12". Done.
7. Check the live summary under the ingredient list, before saving.
8. Save.

### 3.3 Expected numbers

| Where | Must show |
| --- | --- |
| Editor summary (step 7) | `Hydration 77.3% (includes levain)` and `Flour 550 g · Water 425 g · Dough 985 g` |
| Ingredient editor, Bread flour 450 g | "Costs $1.08 in this recipe" |
| Detail screen header stats | Hydration 77.3% (425 g water over 550 g flour), Total dough 985 g |
| Detail totals block | Total flour 550 g, Total water 425 g, Hydration 77.3%, Total dough 985 g |
| Baker's % (of 550 g flour) | Bread flour 81.8%, Whole wheat 9.1%, Water 68.2%, Salt 1.8%, Levain 18.2% |
| Detail cost card | `$1.39 per loaf`, `$1.39 for 1 loaf`, `Suggested price $4.17 per loaf` (default 200% markup) |
| Cost breakdown rows | Bread flour $1.08 (77.7%), Whole wheat flour $0.18 (12.9%), Levain $0.12 (8.6%), Salt $0.01 (0.7%), Water Free ("375 g · Free") |
| Cost breakdown footer | "Ingredient cost + 200% markup", and "Ingredients only" note |

Arithmetic, so a tester can see where each number comes from: 450 g x $0.0024 = $1.08; 50 g x $0.0035 = $0.175, shown as $0.18; 10 g x $0.0010 = $0.01; levain flour 50 g x $0.0024 = $0.12; water $0. Sum in cents 108 + 18 + 1 + 12 = 139. Sell price 139 x 3 = 417 cents.

| Step | Action | Expected |

| 2 | Open the cost card at 2 loaves | Rows $2.16, $0.35, $0.02, $0.24, Free. Footnote: per loaf is the batch total divided by 2, rounded to the nearest $0.01 |
